Who are the best steel fabricators in Madurai?

Sri Krishna Iron Works has been fabricating and building steel structures in Madurai since 1925, with more than 1000 projects completed across Tamil Nadu. We handle design, foundations, fabrication, erection and roofing.

Do you build warehouses and PEB buildings?

Yes. We design and build warehouses, pre-engineered buildings (PEB) and factory sheds, including foundations, steel frames, roof trusses and roofing sheets.

Which areas do you work in?

We are based in Madurai and have completed projects in Madurai, Chennai, Trichy, Tirunelveli, Sivakasi and Dindigul, and across Tamil Nadu.

Do you supply and fix roofing sheets?

Yes. We supply and install roofing and cladding sheets along with steel roof trusses, purlins and gutters.

Can you build mezzanine floors, steel staircases and canopies?

Yes. We fabricate and install mezzanine floors, steel staircases, platforms, walkways and steel canopies for schools, colleges, hospitals and industries.
