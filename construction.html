FAQ

Common questions

Which areas do you serve?

We are based in Madurai and have completed projects across Tamil Nadu, including Madurai, Chennai, Trichy, Tirunelveli, Sivakasi and Dindigul. Contact us with your site location for projects elsewhere.

Do you provide structural design?

Yes. We provide structural design for our buildings, and we can also fabricate and erect from drawings prepared by your own consultant.

Do you handle foundations and civil work?

Yes. We can take up the complete job: foundations and civil work, steel fabrication, erection, and roofing and cladding sheets. That gives you one point of responsibility.

What information do you need for a quotation?

Site location, approximate building length, width and height, intended use, and any drawings you have. Photos of the site are also helpful.

How long does a typical warehouse take?

It depends on size, design approval and site readiness. We provide a project-specific schedule with every quotation.

Planning a warehouse or industrial shed?

Tell us the location, size and use. We will come back with questions, a site visit if needed, and a clear quotation.
