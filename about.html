About us

A Madurai ironworks since 1925.

Sri Krishna Iron Works has worked with iron and steel in Madurai for more than a hundred years. Today we design, fabricate and erect complete steel structures across Tamil Nadu.

Our story

Established in 1925

Sri Krishna Iron Works was established in Madurai in 1925. Over the decades the business has grown from ironwork into structural steel design, fabrication and construction, completing more than 1000 projects across Madurai, Chennai, Trichy, Tirunelveli, Sivakasi and Dindigul. Our work includes industrial sheds, steel canopies, roof structures and steel-framed floors for businesses, schools, colleges and hospitals.

Today the business is run by Ram Kumar K K, Raj Kumar K K and Sanjay Kumar K K R.

Our focus is simple: understand the requirement properly, fabricate accurately from approved drawings, and deliver structures that go together on site without surprises.
